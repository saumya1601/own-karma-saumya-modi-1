# Concept 7: Origami 3D Perspective Fold

A menu that folds open in 3D perspective, slicing the viewport into vertical panels that rotate on a hinge (origami accordion style).

## Design Aesthetic
* **Folded Panels**: Vertical slate/gold folding panels rotating on Y hinges with 3D depth perspective.
* **Staggered Reveals**: Accordion folding/unfolding motion where content slides in behind the panels.

## Library Stack Integration
* **`gsap`**: Controls the `transformPerspective`, `rotationY`, and `stagger` timings to render smooth 3D hinges.
