# Concept 6: Interactive Star Constellation

A constellation map background where nodes (representing menu links) are stars connected by thin golden laser lines that bend, grab, and attract to the user's cursor.

## Design Aesthetic
* **Cosmic Network**: Golden stars drifting inside a dark viewport container.
* **Laser Links**: Fine wireframe lines linking stars dynamically.

## Library Stack Integration
* **`@tsparticles/react` & `@tsparticles/slim`**: Renders the dynamic WebGL canvas star cluster and controls the interactive grab boundaries.
