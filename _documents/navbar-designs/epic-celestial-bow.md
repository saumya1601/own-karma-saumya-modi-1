# Concept 9: Epic Celestial Bow Menu (Ramayana Inspired)

A high-fidelity celestial navigation bar inspired by the cinematic teaser teaser of Namit Malhotra's *Ramayana* epic. It features a bottom-left golden astrolabe bow & arrow launcher that dynamically aims and shoots projectiles across the page to hit a top-right horizontal menu.

## Design Aesthetic

* **Cosmic Mythology**: Dark obsidian space canvas with radial golden aura glow representing standard atmospheric cinema.
* **Separated Geometry**: A horizontal navigation link list sits at the top-right (`absolute top-6 right-6`), and the golden Kodanda bow launcher sits at the bottom-left (`absolute bottom-8 left-8`).
* **Dynamic Real-Time Aiming**: The bow and arrow track cursor hover states and rotate on a pivot in real-time, dynamically targeting the exact coordinates of the active/hovered button.
* **Diagonal Archery Launch**: Clicking a link triggers a 3D-accelerated GSAP flight: pulling the string back, launching the arrow diagonally across the screen, striking the targeted button, and releasing a gold spark particle explosion on impact.
