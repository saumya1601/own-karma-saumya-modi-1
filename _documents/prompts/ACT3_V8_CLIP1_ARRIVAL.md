# ACT III — V8 CLIP 1: ARRIVAL (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
The story opens with the camera approaching the exterior of an ancient interstellar craft — a kilometer-long cathedral-ship drifting silent through the deep black between stars. The hull is real, physical, weathered: dark obsidian plating veined with fine gold seams, patina-green oxidized brass fittings, ribs of ancient carved stone. A single open airlock ahead frames a first glimpse of the interior. The camera glides toward it, closes the distance in silence, and just crosses the threshold as the shot ends. Ends on the frame that seeds Clip 2 — the camera on the inside of the airlock, looking down the length of the ship's cathedral nave.

### V8 principle: **realism, not symbolism**
V6 was Kubrick-minimal. V7 was maximalist archive of drifting symbolic objects. V8 is neither. V8 is a **real place** with real architecture, real materials, real physics, and a story implied by the environment. The camera is discovering an artifact — a lost cathedral-ship the size of a city — not a dreamscape.

Written as a location scout's briefing crossed with a screenwriter's opening slugline. Specific real-world architectural nouns (hull, buttress, airlock, nave, arch, vault) and specific real-world material nouns (obsidian plating, verdigris brass, gold seam, carved basalt) do the heavy lifting instead of atmospheric abstractions.

### Design principles carried from V6 and reinforced for smoothness
- **The camera never changes pace across all three clips.** Same slow forward drift from the first frame of Clip 1 to the final frame of Clip 3. Smoothness comes from continuity of motion, not from the model — the prompt has to explicitly hold pace.
- **Each clip's ending is designed to be visually adjacent to the next clip's beginning** so the seed image transition is seamless.
- **85/10/5 palette lock** still enforced but described in real-world terms (obsidian, gold seam, aged bone-ivory silk) instead of hex codes stuffed into the prose. Hex codes appear once at the bottom as a reference line.
- **Locked forward axis camera** — no shake, no rotation, no orbit, no zoom. Real cinematic tracking, not video-game camera.
- **Affirmative descriptions** — no `do not X` lists.

### COLOR & MATERIAL REFERENCE (single line, kept out of the prompt body)
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the void, the shadow, the base of every dark material. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as aged bone-ivory silk creases and the faintest tint on the smallest highlights. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only in the hull's fine gold seams, the interior's kintsugi veins, and the distant altar light — sparse thin lines, not washes.

### PROMPT (Copy-Paste Below)

```
A silent, ultra-slow cinematic forward drift through the deep vacuum of interstellar space. The camera moves at the pace of a held breath, on a perfectly locked forward axis. No shake, no rotation, no orbit, no zoom, no handheld motion. Cathedral-scale, physical, real.

Ahead of the camera, filling most of the frame, hangs the hull of an ancient interstellar craft — a kilometer-long, cathedral-shaped vessel drifting derelict through the black between stars. The hull is real, physical, and heavily weathered: massive plates of polished dark obsidian riveted together over ribs of carved dark basalt stone, the seams between them running with fine luminous veins of antique gold that pulse faintly, like kintsugi holding the whole vessel together. Along the ship's flank, verdigris-green patina crusts the ancient bronze fittings, oxidized by millennia in the cold. Micrometeorite pockmarks and long slow-motion scratches mar the hull in places, catching thin lines of warm gold rim light along their edges. The ship is silent. Vast. Dead. Beautiful.

Ahead and centered in the frame, a single open airlock breaks the hull's surface — a tall carved-stone arch framed in dark basalt, its interior black except for a distant point of warm gold light glowing softly deep inside the ship. The airlock is the camera's destination.

Around the camera, the deep vacuum: pure black void, softened only by a sparse scattering of small warm-toned pinpoint stars widely spaced across the deep background. A few flecks of fine gold cosmic dust drift weightlessly through the near foreground — physical particulate matter shed by the ancient hull, catching only the smallest amount of warm reflected light. Volumetric dust is thin and sparse, never a glowing haze.

As the camera drifts, the ship's hull grows closer and larger in the frame with real parallax. The gold seams' kintsugi glow reveals more of its fine detail. The airlock's arch widens as the distance closes. In the final moments of the shot, the camera crosses the threshold of the airlock and just enters the ship's interior — the frame now framed on three sides by the ancient carved-stone arch, with the vast interior nave revealing itself ahead in the deep background: two receding rows of massive obsidian columns, distant vaulted ceiling lost in shadow, and a single distant warm gold light glowing at the far end of the nave.

The material detail is exhaustive: the obsidian plating shows its polished reflective grain, the basalt ribs show carved sacred geometry cut deep into the stone, the gold seams show individual liquid-metal filigree, the verdigris brass shows its patina texture, the dust motes show individual particulate mass. Warm gold rim light rakes across every seam, every carved edge, every bronze fitting.

Roughly 85% of every frame stays deep pitch black shadow — the vacuum around the ship, the shadow across the hull's dark plating, the interior visible through the airlock arch. Warm ivory (#F4F0E8) appears only as the faintest tint on the smallest highlights and on the most distant stars, no more than 10% of the frame. Warm antique gold (#C9A55A) appears only as the hull's fine kintsugi seams, the airlock's inner ember, and the distant altar light glimpsed through the arch — sparse thin lines and small points only, no more than 5% of the frame.

Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, 35mm anamorphic lens with subtle horizontal flare on the hull's gold seams and the distant altar light, T2.8 shallow depth of field with parallax on the approaching hull, natural 35mm film grain, thin volumetric particulate in the near foreground, deep vacuum haze in the background, locked forward axis, no camera shake.

Ends on a frame with the camera just inside the airlock, the ancient carved-stone arch framing three sides of the frame, the vast interior nave of the cathedral-ship revealing itself in the deep background — two receding rows of massive obsidian columns, distant vaulted ceiling in shadow, a single small warm gold light glowing at the far end of the nave.
```

### KEY VISUAL NOTES
- **The ship is a real object with a history.** Micrometeorite scars, oxidized brass, carved sacred geometry cut into the stone ribs. Every material detail implies age and use.
- **Parallax on the approaching hull** is the smooth-motion cue — the ship grows in the frame as the camera closes distance. That real parallax is what sells the physicality of the space.
- **The final beat is a threshold crossing** — the camera has just entered the ship as the shot ends. This is the story's first "we're inside" moment, and it seeds Clip 2 with a frame that's already looking down the length of the interior nave.
