# ACT III — V7 CLIP 1: APPROACH TO THE ARCHIVE (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
The opposite of V6's Kubrick-minimal void. Here the camera drifts toward a vast, densely-layered cosmic archive suspended in the black between stars — an interstellar cathedral of floating memories, packed with texture and depth on every layer. Sparse debris drifts near the camera in the foreground. A dense mid-ground cloud of tumbling silk, stone, gold thread, and calligraphic parchment stretches into the distance. Volumetric gold dust suffuses the whole scene. At the far vanishing point, a warm gold ember waits inside the deepest heart of the archive. Ends on the frame that seeds Clip 2.

### V7 principle: **layered density, not clutter**
Flash can render extremely dense scenes when the density is structured across explicit **depth planes** — foreground, mid-ground, background — with different scales and different motions on each. Density fails when everything is at the same depth and scale (Flash averages it into visual soup). This prompt (and the two after it) describe every element by its depth plane, its scale relative to the camera, its motion vector, and its material texture — deliberately over-describing so Flash has enough scaffolding to hang detail on.

### Design principles carried from V4 and V6, still enforced
- **85% black / 10% ivory / 5% gold** palette lock — silk and stone base tones are dark charcoal-black, not pale.
- **Locked forward drift** camera — no shake, no rotation, no orbit, no zoom.
- **Affirmative descriptions** instead of `do not X` lists — Flash inverts negatives.
- **Sequential temporal instructions** ("in the foreground… in the mid-ground… in the deep background…") instead of hard timestamps.

### COLOR & MATERIAL LOCK
- Pitch black `#000000` — the void itself, the deep background, the base fabric of every silk, the base stone of every tablet.
- Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` — only as thin sheen on silk creases, faintest tint on the most distant dust motes and stars, the polished surface of a single parchment fragment. Never a fabric or stone base color.
- Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` — kintsugi veins in stone, calligraphic light, gold thread edges, volumetric dust, the distant heart-ember. Sparse thin lines and small points only, even when there are hundreds of them.
- The darkness reads as velvet depth thick with suspended particulate — not as flat black, not as bright fog.

A silent, ultra-slow cinematic forward drift through the deepest black of interstellar space, moving at the pace of a held breath, on a perfectly locked forward axis. No shake, no rotation, no orbit, no zoom, no handheld motion. Cathedral-scale, weightless, dreamlike.

A slow, densely-woven cloud of fine gold cosmic dust suffuses the entire foreground layer — thousands of pinpoint amber motes drifting in slow zero-gravity paths, each catching a tiny reflection of the others, forming volumetric warm-lit atmosphere without ever brightening the deep black behind it.

MID-GROUND, extending back into the archive: much denser and smaller-scaled. A layered field of dozens of floating memory-fragments at varying depths and orientations — smaller silk banners rippling in slow-motion zero-gravity, tumbling stone slabs of varying size, thin gold line-art sacred geometry symbols (Metatron's Cube and Flower of Life patterns) rotating slowly on their vertical axes, and unfurling calligraphic parchment scrolls with the impression of ancient handwritten script drifting weightlessly between them (only the suggestion of writing, not legible words). Every parchment is faintly translucent, its writing rendered in warm gold calligraphic light. Every gold symbol is small, thin-lined, self-contained, and glows softly without spilling light onto the darkness around it. Between all of them, the same volumetric gold dust drifts continuously.

DEEP BACKGROUND, at the vanishing point: the innermost heart of the archive. A dense atmospheric haze softens everything past a certain depth, dissolving the layered memories into velvet darkness. At the very center of the deep background, a single small warm gold ember glows softly through the haze — the visitor's distant destination, candle-soft, unfocused, framed by the receding layers of floating memories on both sides. Behind and above the archive, a sparse scattering of small warm-toned pinpoint stars glows dimly against the black void, widely spaced.

The material detail is exhaustive across the frame: threads on the silk, striations on the stone, individual gold particles in the dust, calligraphic brushstroke edges on the parchment. Warm gold rim light rakes across every fold, every crack, every symbol edge, catching just the highlights and leaving the rest of every surface deep in shadow. Anamorphic horizontal lens flares glint softly across the gold symbols and the distant ember, and delicate warm bokeh halos form around the closest dust motes.

Roughly 85% of every frame stays deep pitch black shadow. Warm ivory appears only as thin creased sheen on silk and the faintest tint on the most distant highlights, no more than 10% of the frame. Warm antique gold appears as kintsugi veins, thin geometric line-art, calligraphic script light, volumetric dust, and the distant ember — sparse and thin even at high element counts, no more than 5% of the frame.

Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, anamorphic lens with subtle horizontal flare on the gold symbols and the distant ember, T2.8 shallow depth of field with deep bokeh falloff into the mid-ground, natural 35mm film grain, thick volumetric atmospheric haze that reads as deep velvet particulate rather than as bright fog, locked forward axis, no camera shake.

Ends on a frame where the camera is at the outer threshold of the archive — foreground silk banner drifting on the left, foreground stone tablet with gold kintsugi cracks in the lower right, densely-layered field of floating memories receding into hazy mid-ground depth, distant warm gold ember centered at the vanishing point deep inside the archive, sparse warm pinpoint stars scattered behind everything, volumetric gold dust suffusing the whole frame.

### KEY VISUAL NOTES
- **Density is layered, not clumped.** Three explicit depth planes, each with a different scale and motion register.
- **Every foreground element is described with material texture** (silk weft, stone striations, thread edges) so Flash has something to render at close scale.
- **Every mid-ground element is described as one of a category** ("dozens of smaller silk banners… stone slabs of varying size… thin gold line-art symbols") so Flash renders the density as a field of variations rather than duplicates.
- **The palette lock and camera lock are still enforced**, even at high element counts.
