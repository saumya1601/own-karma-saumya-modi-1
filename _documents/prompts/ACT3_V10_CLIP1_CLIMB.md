# ACT III — V10 CLIP 1: THE CLIMB (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
Earthbound, meditative, real. First-person camera walks up a stone-paved mountain pass at dusk on an ancient trade route — a Himalayan silk-road path carved into a dark ridge. Aged stone steps, wind-blown dust off the pass, a single dark silk prayer flag rippling from a weathered wooden post at the trail's edge, sparse first stars appearing in a deepening blue-black sky above. The camera's pace is a real walking pace — human, terrestrial.

### Touchstones
- **Journey (video game)** — walking pace, warm horizon light, silent progression up a ridge.
- **Kurosawa's landscape framing** — a lone traveler dwarfed by a mountain pass.
- **Bhutan / Himalayan documentary** — real stone, real prayer flag, real weathered wood.

### What this clip introduces
- **Stars** (first faint pinpoints in the deepening dusk sky)
- **Dust** (fine wind-blown pass dust drifting across the trail)
- **Stone** (ancient hand-carved trail steps and the ridge itself)
- **Fabric** (a single dark silk prayer flag rippling from a wooden post)

### COLOR LOCK
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the ridge silhouette, the deepening sky, and the trail's shadowed sides. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as faint dusk light on the highest ridge crests and the palest first stars. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only as a thin band of dying sunset light along the western horizon and the prayer flag's hem-stitched gold thread.

```
A silent, ultra-slow cinematic first-person forward walking POV up a narrow stone-paved mountain pass at dusk on an ancient Himalayan silk-road trail. The camera moves at the pace of a contemplative walker's stride, on a perfectly locked forward axis. No shake, no rotation, no zoom, no bounce. Real weathered earth-scale, patient, silent.

Beneath the camera, hand-carved stone steps rise gently up the pass — worn dark basalt slabs polished by a thousand years of feet, their edges rounded, their surfaces cracked with fine dark seams. The steps curve slightly to the right as they climb, following the ridge line.

On both sides of the trail, the ridge falls away into deep shadow. To the left, a sheer dark rock face rises out of frame. To the right, a slow drop into the deep valley below where blue-hour mist has already gathered, thick and dark, obscuring anything past a certain depth. The frame is dominated by shadow — the ridge, the valley mist, the deepening sky above.

At the right edge of the trail, driven into a weathered wooden post, a single tall dark silk prayer flag ripples slowly in the wind — deep charcoal-black raw silk, its lower hem stitched with a thin fine line of antique gold thread that catches the last dying dusk light on its western edge. Most of the flag reads as dark silhouette against the mist behind it; only the hem-stitched gold and the crease highlights on its western face carry warm color.

Above the ridge, filling the upper half of the frame, the deepening dusk sky. Along the western horizon at the far edge of the frame, a thin dying band of warm antique gold marks where the sun has just set — a low horizontal glow along the mountain silhouette, not a bright disc, not a point of light, only a thin line of horizon warmth. Above that, the sky graduates through deep blue-black, and the first faint sparse warm-toned pinpoint stars have begun to appear in the deeper dark overhead — pale, small, widely spaced.

Fine wind-blown dust drifts across the trail from the west — small pinpoint motes of amber-lit particulate caught in the dying horizon light on their western side, dark on their eastern side, drifting slowly across the frame in slow diagonal paths.

The material detail is exhaustive: individual carved seams in the basalt steps, individual weathered grain in the wooden prayer-flag post, individual warp-and-weft silk threads on the flag, individual dust motes catching horizon light, individual mountain silhouette edges cut sharp against the sky. Warm gold light comes only from the low western horizon and is directional — it rakes across west-facing surfaces and leaves east-facing surfaces deep in shadow.

Roughly 85% of every frame stays deep pitch black shadow — the ridge silhouette, the valley mist, the eastern half of every object. Warm ivory (#F4F0E8) appears only as faint dusk sky-tone in the upper third and as the palest tint on the smallest first stars, no more than 10% of the frame. Warm antique gold (#C9A55A) appears only as the thin dying band along the western horizon, the prayer flag's hem-stitched gold thread, the horizon-lit dust motes, and thin rim light on west-facing crease highlights — sparse thin lines and small points only, no more than 5% of the frame.

Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, 35mm anamorphic lens with a very subtle horizontal flare only along the horizon band, T2.8 shallow depth of field, natural 35mm film grain, thick blue-hour valley mist in the deep background, locked forward walking axis, no camera shake.

Ends on a frame: stone trail steps curving to the right leading up the pass, a single dark silk prayer flag rippling from a weathered wooden post on the right edge with hem-stitched gold catching horizon light, a thin dying band of warm antique gold along the western horizon at the frame's left edge, first faint stars in the deepening dusk sky above, blue-hour mist thick in the valley below on the right, rest of the frame deep shadow.
```
