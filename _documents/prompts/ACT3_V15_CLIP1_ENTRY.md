# ACT III — V15 CLIP 1: THE ENTRY (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
Wabi-sabi luxury, monumental Japan. Camera enters through a cathedral-scale sliding shoji screen and glides forward down the length of a monumental Japanese tea room scaled up to enormous proportions. A vast tatami-textured stone floor stretches into deep atmospheric haze. A distant dark iron kettle waits far ahead. Along the sides, hanging silk hanging scrolls and unlit paper lanterns. Through the shoji windows on both sides, sparse warm-toned stars are visible in a deep blue-black night sky beyond. Kyoto meets Kubrick.

### V15 principle: **Japanese aesthetics scaled to cathedral, palette-locked to Own Karma**
Traditional Japanese tea room elements — shoji screens, tatami floor, hanging scrolls, iron kettle, paper lanterns, ink brushwork — but scaled to Kubrickian monumental proportions and locked to the 85/10/5 palette. Everything is deep dark aged materials with warm gold accents only where light catches, never a "brightly-lit tea room."

### Touchstones
- **Kyoto's Ryoan-ji temple** — wabi-sabi restraint at architectural scale.
- **Isamu Noguchi sculpture gardens** — refined monumental Japanese-modernist proportion.
- **Kubo and the Two Strings** — cinematic ink-brush Japanese aesthetic.
- **Kubrick's symmetrical corridor compositions** (The Shining, 2001) — corridor cinematography scaled up.

### What this clip introduces
- **Stars** (visible through the shoji windows on both sides in the deep night sky beyond)
- **Dust** (fine gold cosmic dust in the air, drifting slowly through the hall)
- **Stone** (tatami-textured stone floor and stone walls of the tea room's structure)

### COLOR LOCK
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the shoji screen frames, the tatami-textured stone floor's dark tone, the ceiling shadow, and the space between light sources. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as the shoji screens' aged paper panels catching only the palest moonlight from behind and the faintest tint on the smallest stars. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only as the unlit paper lantern rims catching moonlight, thin ink-brush gold accents on the walls, and drifting dust catching light.
