### Concept
The camera passes through the gap. The dark silk sails begin to fray at their edges — individual threads lifting free, catching gold light as they rise, and dissolving into a hushed, sparse field of stars. Cloth unraveling into cosmos, still contained within the corridor, still mostly black. The still water below mirrors it.

### COLOR LOCK (carried over from Clip 1 — do not drift toward brighter)
- `#000000` pure black — **~85% of the frame.** Water, shadow, negative space, and the base of any remaining silk.
- `#F4F0E8` warm ivory — **~10% at most.** Only as thin sheen on fabric creases and the faintest warm tint on the smallest stars — never a bright, evenly-lit surface.
- `#C9A55A` antique gold — **~5%.** Sparse thread-light and dust only. Never a glow that fills a large area of frame.
- **The starfield must stay sparse and dim** — small, scattered points, not a bright nebula or glowing haze. If the stars start to read as a bright wash, there are too many of them or they are too large; keep them pinpoint-sized and spaced apart.
- **No bright fog, no daylight-white light, no blue or green tint.**
