# ACT III — V14 CLIP 1: THE APPROACH (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
Deep-space cinematic. Camera drifts slowly through the vacuum toward a vast dark celestial body — a lightless planet or moon — silhouetted against a distant unseen sun far behind it. The sun is completely occluded by the body; only a thin gold "diamond ring" of light rings the body's silhouette along one crescent edge. The body is monumental in the frame — its silhouette curves through the right two-thirds of the frame, its rim light along its western crescent providing all directional lighting for the scene. In the vacuum around it, sparse memories drift silently: a single dark silk banner far in the left of the frame, sparse gold dust catching the ring light on their western sides.

### V14 principle: **the light source is a ring, not a dot**
Every previous "warm gold light source" in the sequence has been either a dot (V4-V8's altar ember), a horizon band (V9's off-screen sun), or off-screen entirely (V6-V9). V14's light is a **thin gold crescent ring** wrapping around the silhouette of a dark celestial body — the sun is *behind* the body, completely eclipsed, and only its bent-light corona is visible as a curving filigree line. This is Interstellar's Gargantua approach reversed for restraint.

### Touchstones
- **Interstellar's Gargantua approach sequence** — but darker, without the accretion disk.
- **Apollo 8 Earthrise footage** — the way a dark planetary body has monumental presence in the vacuum.
- **2001: A Space Odyssey's monolith-and-moon compositions** — geometric restraint.

### What this clip introduces
- **Stars** (sparse warm-toned pinpoint stars visible in the left third of the deep background void, never overlapping the eclipsed body)
- **Dust** (sparse gold cosmic dust drifting in the vacuum, catching ring light on western sides)
- **Fabric** (a single dark silk banner drifting far in the left of the frame)

### COLOR LOCK
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the void, the eclipsed body's silhouette, and everything past visibility. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as the faintest tint on the smallest, most distant stars. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only as the thin crescent ring of eclipse corona along the body's western edge, dust motes catching the ring light on their western sides, and hem-stitched thread on the silk banner.
