### Concept
The camera passes the first colossal monolith and drifts into a cathedral-like space between two even larger monoliths flanking the frame. Above the camera, a vast monumental stone arch bridges the two forms — a Dune-scale cosmic structure suspended in vacuum. Beyond the arch, in the deep background, the horizon light continues to rake across the tops of the monoliths from off-screen left. Below the camera at the base of the space, the second monolith from Clip 1 is now revealed as a fallen slab drifting horizontally in the void, its own kintsugi cracks catching the horizon light. Between the two flanking monoliths and the arch, drifting weightless, tall dark silk banners of an ancient civilization hang preserved by vacuum — the first Journey-echo of cloth against monumental architecture. Ends on the frame that seeds Clip 3.

### Touchstones informing this clip
- **Dune (2021)** — the throne-room register: monumental stone architecture, camera drifting through a cathedral-scale chamber, deep atmospheric haze softening the far ends of the space, silence-and-scale as the emotional register.
- **Interstellar** — the vacuum silence, the atmospheric perspective through cathedral distances, the sense of the camera being tiny inside something vast.
- **Journey (video game)** — the tall drifting silk banners against monumental stone. Journey's Cathedral Chamber has exactly this feeling of cloth catching directional light against colossal stone walls.
- **Apple Vision Pro / ManvsMachine** — the material fidelity on every surface (obsidian polish, basalt grain, silk warp/weft, kintsugi filigree) and the buttery-smooth cinematic parallax as the camera enters the cathedral space.

### V9 principle: **monumentality through parallax**
The camera's forward drift is what sells the scale. As the camera advances, the near monoliths pass the camera's shoulders with real cinematic parallax, the arch above passes overhead, and the fallen slab below passes beneath — while the distant background structures barely move. That parallax difference across depth planes is the Interstellar-scale cue. Flash renders it cleanly when the depth planes are described explicitly with different scales and different apparent motions.
