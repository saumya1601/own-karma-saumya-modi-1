### Concept
The camera reaches the far end of the tea-room hall and arrives at the dark iron kettle on its low platform. Beside the kettle rests a shallow dark stone tea basin holding still black water. The camera decelerates and comes to rest before the platform. The kettle's lid slowly lifts of its own accord — no visible hand. From within, a single thin thread of warm gold steam rises vertically through the frame — the Act V thread echo as rising tea steam. It rises steadily, curling gently, at meditation pace. It reaches the coffered ceiling above and disperses into small dim gold motes. Then the moonlight from the shoji panels behind the kettle begins dimming — the moon has set, or a cloud has passed over. Blue-black night thickens through the shoji screens. The dust and remaining incense smoke settle. Everything fades to near-black.

### V15 principle in Clip 3: **the steam is the thread; the moon setting is the dissolve**
The Act V golden-thread echo is a rising thread of gold-white tea steam from a kettle — a real physical thread with real physical behavior. The dissolve is the moonlight from the shoji panels dimming as the moon sets or clouds pass over, not mist rising from below. It's the tea room returning to full night stillness.
