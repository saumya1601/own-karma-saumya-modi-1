### Concept
The camera crosses the rope bridge and reaches the deepest chamber of the cave cathedral, where all the kintsugi mineral veins converge. In the center of the floor, a still black pool of glassy dark water — a subterranean tarn fed by centuries of drips from the ceiling above. The pool is mirror-perfect. The camera comes to rest at its edge. From the vaulted ceiling above, a single drip of mineralized water descends — but instead of water, it's a thin thread of warm liquid gold, drawing itself downward through the frame at meditation pace like slow honey falling from a stalactite. Then thick dark cave mist begins rising from the water's surface and rolling outward through the chamber, consuming everything. Frame settles into deep near-black — seamless handoff to Act IV.

The thread doesn't hover; it descends. The dissolve is cave mist rising from the disturbed pool — real geology of a subterranean spring venting.

They cross the bridge.
They reach the deepest chamber, where a still black pool waits at the center of the floor.
They stop.

The pool ripples once.
Cave mist rises from the disturbed spring and takes the cavern back into darkness.
