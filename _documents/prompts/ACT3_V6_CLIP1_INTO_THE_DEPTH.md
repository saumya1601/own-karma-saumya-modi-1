# ACT III — V6 CLIP 1: INTO THE DEPTH (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
The camera drifts forward into the deepest part of space, between stars, where there is no destination and no light — only stillness. For most of the shot, the frame is almost entirely black. Slowly, gold dust appears in the darkness. Then, at the very edge of the vanishing point, a single warm gold ember reveals itself. That's it. No sea, no columns, no banners, no monoliths. Only void, dust, and one distant point of warmth. Ends on the frame that seeds Clip 2.

### What changed from V5 — and why
V5 tried to establish the entire corridor architecture in the first frame (cosmic sea + silk banner + stone monolith + ember + stars + dust all at once). That's how landscape paintings work, not how cinema works. Great opening shots reveal one thing at a time. V6 gives Clip 1 a single subject — **the void itself** — and lets everything else arrive in Clips 2 and 3.

Other structural changes on Flash's terms:
- **One focal element per shot**, not five. Flash renders singular imagery beautifully and muddles overloaded frames.
- **Prompt opens with the camera and the mood**, not a technical palette lock. Flash weights the opening most heavily; leading with hex codes wastes that weight.
- **Palette stated once, briefly, in the middle** — not restated top and bottom.
- **No lists of `do not X, do not Y, do not Z`.** Flash fixates on negatives and inverts them. Instead, this prompt affirmatively describes what the darkness looks like.
- **The tone is direction, not specification.** Poetic, spare, cinematic. Written the way a DP briefs a camera operator, not the way an engineer writes a JIRA ticket.

### COLOR LOCK (read once — you won't see this in the prompt body)
Pitch black `#000000` dominates ~85% of the frame. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` never appears as a surface, only as the faintest tint on the smallest dust motes at the very edge of the light. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` is present only as sparse dust and the distant ember. Everything else is void.

### PROMPT (Copy-Paste Below)

```
A silent, ultra-slow forward drift through the deepest black of interstellar space. The camera moves at the pace of a held breath, on a perfectly locked forward axis. No shake, no rotation, no orbit, no zoom. The frame is almost entirely black.

For the first few beats, there is only darkness. No stars, no objects, no horizon — just the pure, deep black of the void, softened by the faintest atmospheric haze. Kubrick-slow. Villeneuve-quiet.

Then, without warning, fine gold cosmic dust begins to drift into view — sparse, weightless, pinpoint-sized motes of warm amber light suspended in zero gravity. They do not fill the frame. They drift in slow zero-gravity paths, a few at a time, catching the smallest amount of warm light on their surfaces. Between them, the void remains deep and black.

As the camera continues its drift, a single warm gold ember slowly reveals itself far ahead at the vanishing point of the frame — soft, unfocused, candle-small, glowing through the dark haze like a distant window in a cathedral. It is the only source of concentrated light in the entire frame, and it is small. The camera never reaches it.

The color palette is deep pitch black (#000000) for the vast majority of the frame, with warm antique gold (#C9A55A) only in the sparse drifting dust and the tiny distant ember. Warm ivory (#F4F0E8) appears only as the faintest possible tint on the smallest, most distant dust motes — never as a surface. The darkness feels like velvet, not like fog.

Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, anamorphic lens, T2.8 shallow depth of field, a very subtle horizontal flare only on the distant ember, natural 35mm film grain, dark cosmic haze that reads as depth rather than brightness, locked forward axis, no camera shake.

Ends on a frame with the tiny distant warm gold ember centered at the vanishing point, sparse gold dust drifting weightlessly around the camera, and everything else deep, pure black void.
```

### KEY VISUAL NOTES
- **Frame 1 is almost empty.** That's the point. Cinema opens with stillness.
- **The single subject is the darkness itself.** The dust and ember exist to give the darkness scale, not to compete with it for attention.
- **"Velvet, not fog"** is the mental image for the atmospheric haze — depth, not brightness.
- **Camera**: Locked forward drift, no orbit, no rotation, no zoom.

### END-FRAME (Handoff to Clip 2)
Tiny distant warm gold ember centered at the vanishing point · sparse gold cosmic dust drifting weightlessly around the camera · rest of the frame deep, pure black void with a hint of dark atmospheric haze in the deep background.
