### Concept
The camera falls deeper. The shaft widens into a broader vertical space — no longer just a well, but a cathedral of falling. The walls recede outward on all sides. Between the camera and the receding walls, more memory-objects float suspended at various depths: sacred geometry glyphs carved deep into the stone tablets embedded in the walls, aged parchment scrolls fluttering as they fall (some rising past the camera because the camera falls faster than they do; others falling faster than the camera and pulling ahead below). The circle of sky above has grown smaller. The bottom of the shaft is still not visible.

### V13 principle: **relative velocity is the whole visual**
Different objects fall at different rates. Some drift *upward* through the frame (they are stationary; the camera is falling past them). Some fall *downward* through the frame faster than the camera (they are falling faster and pulling ahead below). Some drift *horizontally* (caught in air currents inside the shaft). Flash needs each object's relative motion stated explicitly.
