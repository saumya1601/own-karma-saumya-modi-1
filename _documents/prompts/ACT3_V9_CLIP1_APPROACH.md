# ACT III — V9 CLIP 1: THE APPROACH (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
The camera drifts as first-person POV into a region of the deep cosmos where the void is broken by monumental architecture — a single colossal dark monolith rising out of the black ahead, cathedral-scale, silent, drifting weightless in vacuum. Off-screen to the left, a distant unseen source of warm gold light — a horizon that isn't a horizon — rakes rim-light across the monolith's upper edge and along its left flank. The rest of the monolith stays deep in shadow. The vacuum around the camera is filled with sparse volumetric dust that only catches the horizon light on its illuminated side. Ends on the frame that seeds Clip 2.

### Touchstones informing this clip
- **Interstellar** — the sense of monumental scale in vacuum silence; the camera dwarfed by a single vast object in the frame; the atmospheric perspective of things visible through deep haze at cathedral distance.
- **Dune (2021)** — the monolith's material register: weathered dark stone at architectural scale, warm-gold-hued directional light like Arrakis sunrise raking across the top edge of a monumental form.
- **Journey (video game)** — the emotional use of directional warm light coming from an unseen horizon. The camera is not walking toward a bright point at the vanishing point; it is walking through a landscape *lit* by a distant unseen source. Light is a directional atmosphere, not a destination beacon.
- **Playdead's Inside** — restrained storytelling through silhouette. The monolith is defined by its rim light, not by illuminated surfaces. What we don't see is more of the composition than what we do see.
- **The Mill / ManvsMachine** — cinematic material precision. Real weathering, real stone grain, real particulate physics.
- **Apple Vision Pro product experience** — refined material fidelity, buttery smooth camera motion, considered depth of field.

### Design principles carried from V6, V7, V8 (still enforced)
- **Locked forward drift** camera — no shake, no rotation, no orbit, no zoom.
- **Same slow contemplative pace** held across all 30 seconds.
- **85/10/5 palette lock** enforced in real-world material terms.
- **Affirmative descriptions** — no `do not X` lists.
- **Sequential temporal instructions** — no hard timestamps.

### COLOR & LIGHT REFERENCE (single line, kept out of the prompt body)
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the void and the shadowed side of every material. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as the faintest tint on the smallest highlights. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only as directional rim light from off-screen left along the monolith's upper edge, thin filigree in kintsugi cracks, and horizon-lit particulate.

### KEY VISUAL NOTES
- **The monolith is monumental — kilometers tall — and dwarfs the camera.** That scale is the Interstellar cue. The camera reads as tiny against the vast object.
- **The warm light is directional, from an off-screen horizon** — it isn't a bright dot at the vanishing point. It rakes across the top edge and left flank of the monolith like Arrakis sunrise, defining the form through rim light. The bright *source* stays out of frame.
- **The second distant monolith in the background** is a scale cue — it tells the viewer this region of space is filled with monumental architecture, not a single isolated object.
- **Silhouette dominates.** The monolith is defined by its rim light, not by illuminated surfaces. Playdead register.
