# ACT III — V12 CLIP 1: THE ENTRY (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
Real geology. Camera enters a vast dark limestone cavern deep beneath the earth. Kintsugi at planetary scale — mineral veins of antique gold running through the walls where aeons of water have deposited it. Fine cave dust drifts downward through the frame under real gravity. Sparse warm-toned bioluminescent glowworms cluster on the highest points of the vaulted ceiling — the "starfield" element rendered as real earth biology (Waitomo Caves, New Zealand). No fantasy, no cosmic — just real earth deep in geological time.

### V12 principle: **planetary-scale kintsugi**
Instead of kintsugi on individual stone objects, the entire cave is kintsugi at cathedral scale — long luminous mineral veins of antique gold running through kilometers of dark limestone walls, deposited by water over aeons. Real geology, real material. The Own Karma palette locked in stone.

### Touchstones
- **Herzog's *Cave of Forgotten Dreams*** — the reverence of a filmed cave interior, torchlight on ancient stone.
- **Postojna Caves / Waitomo Caves** — real geology and real bioluminescent starfield.
- **The Descent's** cavern establishing shots (before the horror) — vertical scale and monumental darkness.

### What this clip introduces
- **Stone** (the cavern itself — walls, floor, stalactites)
- **Dust** (fine cave dust drifting downward through the frame)
- **Stars** (as bioluminescent glowworms clustered on the highest points of the vaulted ceiling — real earth biology)

### COLOR LOCK
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the cavern's deep shadow, ceiling darkness, and the unlit majority of every wall. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as the faintest tint on the palest glowworms. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only as the long mineral kintsugi veins in the cave walls, the faintest warm rim on the highest stalactite tips, and the cave dust nearest lit veins.
