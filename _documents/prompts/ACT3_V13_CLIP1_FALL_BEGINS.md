# ACT III — V13 CLIP 1: THE FALL BEGINS (OMNI FLASH)
## Google Flow / Veo — Omni Flash — 10 Second Video (Text-to-Video)

### Concept
Vertical motion, radically different. The camera falls slowly downward through a dark bottomless shaft — a well, a mine, a cosmic chimney. The "walls" of the shaft are made of drifting memories at every depth. As the camera falls, silk banners drift past on the way down, sparse warm-toned stars remain visible looking up above the falling camera, and rising dust particles stream past the camera as gravity carries them opposite to its fall. The first stone tablets embedded in the shaft's walls are just becoming visible on the way down.

### V13 principle: **vertical motion, reversed particulate**
Every previous version has been forward drift. V13 is downward fall. The camera is descending, so:
- Fixed objects (banners, tablets) drift *upward* through the frame as the camera falls past them
- Particulate (dust) rises through the frame — because gravity pulls the camera down faster than dust falls
- Stars visible in the *upper* edge of the frame (looking back up out of the shaft's mouth)
- Deep atmospheric darkening *below* the camera, not ahead of it

Flash needs this stated explicitly because its default is always horizontal forward motion.

### Touchstones
- **Alice's rabbit hole** — the vertical descent through memory.
- **Kubrick's Stargate sequence** (but dark) — the sense of falling through infinite layered space.
- **The bottom of a mine shaft at night** — real vertical geology.

### What this clip introduces
- **Fabric** (silk banners drifting past the camera as it falls)
- **Stars** (visible in the upper edge of the frame looking back up out of the shaft's mouth)
- **Dust** (rising past the camera as it falls)
- **Stone** (the walls of the shaft, first tablets embedded in them)

### COLOR LOCK
Deep pitch black `#000000` for the shaft walls, the darkness below, and everything past visibility. Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` only as thin sheen on silk creases and faintest tint on distant stars. Warm antique gold `#C9A55A` only as kintsugi cracks in the shaft's stone tablets, hem-stitched thread on the silk banners, and rising dust catching the vein light.
