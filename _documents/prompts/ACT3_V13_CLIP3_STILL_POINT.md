### Concept
The fall slows. The camera decelerates continuously and comes to rest hovering just above a still black water pool at the bottom of the shaft — a subterranean tarn at the base of a well of memories. The last of the falling parchments settle onto the water's surface. The rising dust thins as the camera stops. From above the camera — descending vertically from the receding shaft mouth far above through the entire vertical space of the shaft — a single fine thread of warm gold descends, drawn downward at meditation pace, catching kintsugi vein light on its way down as it passes through the shaft. It reaches the camera's still position hovering above the pool and continues past the camera to touch the water below. The pool ripples once. Then dark cave mist begins rising from the pool's surface and thickens outward through the shaft, consuming everything. Frame settles into deep near-black — seamless handoff to Act IV.

### V13 principle in Clip 3: **the fall resolves into stillness at the bottom**
The camera has been falling for 20 seconds. In Clip 3, its fall decays continuously to a complete stop — the still point at the bottom of the well. The descending gold thread is the Act V echo, and it descends *through the whole shaft* from the receding mouth above, passing the camera at rest and touching the water below. The dissolve is dark mist rising from the disturbed water at the bottom.
