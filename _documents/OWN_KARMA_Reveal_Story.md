# OWN KARMA — 30 Second Reveal Story

## Title: *The Circle*

**Total runtime:** 30.000 seconds
**Frame rate:** 24 fps (720 total frames)
**Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 (cinematic anamorphic) — safe-cropped to 16:9 and 9:16 for social
**Color pipeline:** ACES, log-encoded, graded to deep obsidian black + single warm gold (#C9A24B / #E8C87A highlight)

## TYPOGRAPHY REVEAL FORMULA

- **OWN** letters: 267 ms per letter fade-in, ease-out cubic, opacity 0 → 100%.
- **KARMA** letters: 120 ms per letter, same curve — the acceleration makes the second word feel inevitable.
- No slide, no scale, no glow — pure opacity. Anything else breaks the "engraved on the void" feel.
- Tagline *unbound.* fades in over 300 ms, opacity 0 → 60%.
