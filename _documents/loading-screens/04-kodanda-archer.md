# Loader 04 — The Kodanda Archer (Ramayana Inspired)

## Concept

A highly dramatic loader inspired by Namit Malhotra's *Ramayana* cinematic aesthetic. A golden Kodanda bow lies horizontally at the center. As the page loads, the string is drawn back by an invisible hand. When loading hits 100%, the bow fires a blazing golden arrow that tears through the screen, clearing the loading mask to reveal the home experience.

## Visual Description

- **Background**: Matte black space canvas with soft nebulous clouds and faint stars.
- **Center Element**: A magnificent golden bow (`#D4AF37`) oriented horizontally.
- **Tension Loading**: The string bends backwards while a golden arrow fades in, locking into the string.
- **Energy Build**: Sparks and golden dust gather around the arrowhead, intensifying as progress nears 100%.
- **Release**: The arrow fires straight up or forward, leaving a massive golden light trail that wipes the screen clean.

## Typography

- Above the bow: `OWN KARMA // ARCHIVES` (8px mono, tracking 0.25em, opacity 0.4).
- Below the bow: `TENSION: {progress}%` in small amber text.

## Animation Timeline

1. **0–1.0s**: Faint golden bow fades in at center; string is straight.
2. **1.0–5.0s**: The string pulls back continuously, synchronized with the loading progress (e.g. `nockY` moves from 50 to 95). Arrowhead starts glowing.
3. **5.0s (100% Loaded)**: High-speed release. The arrow shoots upwards, the string snaps back with elastic vibration.
4. **5.1–5.5s**: A vertical screen-wide golden light beam sweeps across the page, serving as the route transition transition.

## Motion Principle

Slow Accumulation → High Tension → Instant Release. The animation represents focus and raw potential energy.

## Progress Indicator

The string's mechanical tension and the scale of the glowing arrowhead aura directly represent the loading state from `0%` to `100%`.
