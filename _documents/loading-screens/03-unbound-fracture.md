# Loader 03 — The Unbound Fracture

## Concept

Inspired by Section 04 "THE UNBOUND" from the creative brief. The word **BOUND** is rendered at the center in alabaster white. As loading progresses, the letters fracture and dissolve into golden particles, travel through negative space, and reconstruct as **UNBOUND**.

## Visual Description

- **Background**: Deep obsidian black (`#020617`).
- **Initial State**: The word **BOUND** appears in clean, sharp, uppercase white typography.
- **Fracturing Action**: The individual letterforms split into fine vector shards and tiny gold particles.
- **Drift Phase**: The particles drift in a fluid motion across the screen, guided by a slow coordinate wave.
- **Reconstruction**: The particles converge back in the center, rebuilding themselves line by line into the word **UNBOUND**.
- **Gold Accents**: The prefix **UN** and the particle trails glow in warm molten gold (`#D4AF37`).

## Typography

- Main text: `BOUND` → `UNBOUND` (size 2.5rem, weight 300, tracking 0.4em).
- Font: Elegant sans-serif (e.g., Inter / Outfit).
- Progress details: `0%` to `100%` counter rendered in small golden mono text below the word.

## Animation Timeline

1. **0–1.5s**: The word **BOUND** fades in and remains still.
2. **1.5–3.5s**: Letters begin to fracture into golden dust, drifting outwards.
3. **3.5–5.5s**: The particles cluster and float, representing the transition phase ("inspired by everything").
4. **5.5–7.0s**: Particles gather back in the center to reconstruct as **UNBOUND**.
5. **On Load Complete**: The word glows, expands slightly, and dissolves into the landing page.

## Motion Principle

Stillness → Dissolution → Fluid Transition → Reassembly. The animation highlights the transition from confinement to freedom, matching the core brand ethos: *"Not bound by culture. Not bound by mythology. Inspired by everything. Defined by nothing."*

## Progress Indicator

The numerical loading percentage (`0%` to `100%`) controls the physical transition. At `0-30%` the word is whole; at `30-70%` it is fractured into drifting dust; at `70-100%` it reconstructs into the final word.
