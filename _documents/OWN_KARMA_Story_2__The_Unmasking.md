## WHY THIS STOPS THE SCROLL

Every beat is engineered against a psychological trigger that stops a thumb mid-scroll:

| Trigger | Where it hits | Why it works |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| **Human face / eye contact** | 0.0 – 3.0 s | The human eye is neurologically wired to lock on to another face within 200 ms. An obsidian mask staring dead into camera creates that same fixation without needing a real person. |
| **The break** (first crack) | 3.5 s | A sudden hairline crack across a still face is a visceral "damage" signal. Attention snaps. |
| **Golden light bleeding from within** | 6 – 10 s | Warm light escaping from a dark surface reads as *sacred* — instant emotional weight. |
| **Explosion in slow motion** | 13 – 16 s | Slow-motion destruction is one of the most reliably-watched motions on the internet. |
| **Scale reversal** | 17 – 22 s | Cuts from claustrophobic close-up to infinite cosmos. The brain does a double-take. |
| **Everything collapses to a point** | 23 – 26 s | Inversion of expansion — a rare visual grammar, holds the eye for the payoff. |
| **The line that names it** | 27 – 30 s | Wordmark + a single sentence that reframes the whole film retroactively. |

The film has a hook every 3 – 4 seconds. It's impossible to look away.

## BEAT 01 — THE STILLNESS (00.000 – 03.000)

**Scene:** Extreme close-up of an obsidian ceremonial mask, dead centre, staring directly into camera. Eyes closed. Absolute stillness.

**Camera:** Locked. 85 mm portrait lens equivalent, T2.8. No motion.
**Light:** Single low-key top-left rim light. 90% of the mask is deep shadow. Only the ridge of the brow, cheekbone, and lip catches a razor-thin highlight.
**Element:** Hand-carved obsidian mask, faintly veined with gold like natural marbling. Faceless expression — androgynous, ancient, unreadable. Reference: Mesoamerican, Cycladic, Noh, and Ife bronze all fused.

| Time (ms) | Frame | Action | Audio |
|-----------|-------|--------|-------|
| 0 – 100 | 1 – 2 | Cold open on the mask. No fade-in. Instant presence. | Total silence. |
| 100 – 800 | 3 – 19 | Mask holds. Nothing moves. Not even a dust particle. | 45 Hz sub-drone fades in from silence. |
| 800 – 2000 | 20 – 48 | The gold veins in the stone very faintly pulse once, like a heartbeat inside. | Sub-drone + a single distant heartbeat at 1200 ms. |
| 2000 – 3000 | 49 – 72 | Camera stays locked. Something is about to happen — the frame is holding its breath. | Drone builds by +3 dB. Faint breath layered under. |

**Hook function:** the human brain interprets the mask as a face and locks on. The unnatural stillness makes the viewer wait for the eyes to open.

## GLOBAL VISUAL RULES (locked)

1. Palette: obsidian black `#050505` + molten gold `#C9A24B` / `#E8C87A`. Nothing else.
2. First 3 seconds: hard-locked camera on the mask. No motion at all. The stillness is the hook.
3. Every explosion / expansion / collapse is shot in slow motion. Nothing plays at real time.
4. Sacred geometry patterns must be recognisable (Flower of Life, Fibonacci, Vegvisir) — not generic damage.
5. No people appear at any point. The mask is the only "face". It is stone.
6. Debris is obsidian + gold only. No colour bleeds in.
7. Text appears only in the final 3 seconds. Rendered in post over pure black for pixel-perfect typography.
8. First frame is the mask filling the frame. Last frame is pure black with wordmark. Both frames must be graphically strong enough to work as a standalone thumbnail.
